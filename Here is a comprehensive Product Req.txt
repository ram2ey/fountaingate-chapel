Here is a comprehensive Product Requirements Document (PRD) & Feature Matrix designed specifically for your Vercel + Supabase tech stack.This covers the entire ecosystem: Pastoral Care, Member Management, Facebook/Audio Media Hub, Communications, and Financials.1. Core Platform & Identity System1.1 Authentication & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)Supabase Auth Integration: Email/Password, Magic Link, and Google OAuth logins.Role Permissions:Super Admin / Senior Pastor: Full read/write/delete access across all modules, branch settings, and financial records.Care Leader / Assistant Pastor: Access to Member records, Attendance, Care Notes, and Prayer Requests. (Restricted from financial ledgers).Media / Tech Team: Access to Sermon Hub, Facebook embeds, and MP3 uploader.Cell / Small Group Leader: View-only for assigned cell group members; permission to take cell attendance.Regular Member (PWA Portal): View personal profile, submit prayer requests, stream sermons, and give online.1.2 Multi-Branch / Campus HierarchyBranch Switcher: Top-level dropdown allowing regional overseers to toggle between main campus and satellite branches.Data Isolation: Row-Level Security (RLS) rules ensuring staff only view data belonging to their specific branch ID.2. Member & Visitor Management2.1 Central Member DirectoryComprehensive Profile Fields: Full name, phone, email, date of birth, anniversary, baptism status, home address, and assigned cell group.Family Tree / Household Grouping: Link members under a single "Household Head" for unified care and giving receipts.Custom Tags & Groups: Dynamic tagging (e.g., Choir, Usher, New Believer, Youth).2.2 First-Time Guest & Retention PipelineDigital Guest Intake Form: Mobile-friendly form (or QR code scan) for first-timers to fill out during service.Automated Retention Workflow:Day 1: Automated welcome SMS/WhatsApp via Twilio/Termii.Day 3: Task assigned to care team for a follow-up phone call.Day 7: Invitation sent for New Converts / Membership Class.3. Proactive Pastoral Care & Attendance3.1 Attendance & "At-Risk" TrackingService Check-In: Rapid multi-member check-in interface for Sunday services, mid-week meetings, and small groups.Automated "At-Risk" Engine: Database query dynamically flagging any active member who hasn't attended service or cell group in 3+ consecutive weeks.Intervention Queue: A dedicated dashboard for pastors listing all "At-Risk" members with a 1-click "Reach out via WhatsApp" or "Assign Care Visit" action.3.2 Confidential Care & Visitation NotesPastoral Log: Record notes from counseling sessions, hospital visits, or telephone check-ins.Privacy Toggles: Flag notes as Encrypted/Confidential so only ordained pastors (not general admin staff) can view them.Care Milestone Reminders: Automatic notifications for pastors when a member’s birthday or anniversary is coming up.4. Sermon & Media Ecosystem (Facebook + Audio Storage)4.1 Live Service & Replay HubFacebook Live Auto-Embed: Input the Facebook Video URL to render an inline, responsive Facebook video player with live chat or replay capability.Live Service Alert Banner: A dynamic site-wide banner that automatically activates when is_live = true in Supabase.4.2 On-Demand Audio LibraryDrag-and-Drop MP3 Uploader: Upload audio files directly from the browser to Supabase Storage or Cloudflare R2.Inline HTML5 Audio Player: Minimalist, mobile-friendly media bar allowing members to play sermon audio in the background on their phones.Message Metadata & Search: Filter messages by Speaker, Sermon Series, Scripture Reference, and Date.4.3 Interactive Care Integration"Ask Pastor / Prayer" Overlay: A floating button underneath embedded Facebook streams where viewers can submit private prayer requests while watching.5. Direct Member Communication (WhatsApp & SMS)5.1 Multi-Channel BroadcastsWhatsApp API / SMS Broadcasts: Send bulk notices to targeted groups (e.g., send an SMS to all Ushers or a WhatsApp broadcast to all Cell Leaders).Pre-Formatted Message Templates: Save templates for Sunday reminders, condolence messages, or meeting notifications.1-Click WhatsApp Direct (wa.me links): Click any member's phone number on the dashboard to immediately open a direct WhatsApp chat window.6. Financial Management & Giving6.1 Giving & Tithe LedgerManual & Auto-Entry: Record cash/POS tithes, offerings, building fund pledges, and special donations manually or via online checkout.Local Payment Gateway Links: Direct integration with checkout links (Paystack, Flutterwave, Stripe) for mobile money (MoMo) and card payments.Donor History & Tax Statements: View individual giving records and generate one-click annual PDF contribution statements for tax purposes.7.
